No. Messages arrive on normal WhatsApp — the app parents already use every day. The parent portal also works in any mobile browser, no installation needed.

Just a phone, tablet or computer with internet. For gate scanning, a simple scanner gun (or any phone camera) works. A TV for the live display is optional.

We do the initial setup with you — students, classes, and fee structure — and train your staff. That's included, with no installation charges.
